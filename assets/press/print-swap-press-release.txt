PRESS RELEASE — PRINT SWAP

No buying. No selling. Just the trade.

A non-commercial platform where photographers and visual artists swap
gallery-quality physical prints. Live on iOS.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

"Last year, staring at an empty space on my wall, it struck me that I
should have swapped more prints with photographers over the years. Like
most photographers, I want to be surrounded by work that inspires and
moves me, and that's very seldom my own work."

Photographs were once objects: prints you made, kept in boxes, put on
walls. Print Swap makes that exchange simple again, because good images
deserve more than a few seconds in a feed.

— Sam Faulkner, Founder, Print Swap

----------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW IT WORKS

Upload        Add prints from your portfolio. See what other artists
              are offering.

The exchange  Offer a print. If they say yes, both go into production.

Direct trade  No sales. No commissions. No popularity contests.

Fulfilment    Every print is made to gallery standard, then sent
              straight to the artist.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

NOTES FOR EDITORS

Print Swap soft launched in May 2026 and is now open worldwide. Nearly
300 photographers and visual artists have joined so far. Prints are
produced and shipped from three hubs, in London, Hamburg and New York.

Print Swap was founded by photographer Sam Faulkner (samfaulkner.co.uk).
