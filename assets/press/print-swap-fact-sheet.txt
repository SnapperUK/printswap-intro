WHAT IT IS
A non-commercial platform where photographers and visual artists swap
gallery-quality physical prints. Live on iOS.

HOW IT WORKS
Upload work from your portfolio, see what other artists are offering,
and propose a swap. If the other artist agrees, both prints go into
production and are sent directly to each artist. No sales, no
commissions, no follower counts.

KEY FACTS
- Launch: soft launched May 2026, now open worldwide.
- Community: nearly 300 photographers and visual artists have joined so far.
- Fulfilment: prints are produced and shipped from three hubs —
  London, Hamburg and New York.
- Platform: iOS. App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6752863046
- Founder: photographer Sam Faulkner (samfaulkner.co.uk).

WHAT PRINT SWAP IS NOT
- Not a marketplace. Photographers and visual artists are not selling work.
- Not a social platform. No follower counts, engagement metrics, or
  algorithmic feeds.
- Not a print-on-demand service. The exchange is the point, not the
  transaction.
- Not a startup story. A photographer built a tool for photographers
  and visual artists.
