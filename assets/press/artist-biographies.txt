PRINT SWAP - FEATURED ARTIST BIOGRAPHIES

Last updated: 23 June 2026

These short biographies accompany the selected artist artworks included
in the Print Swap press kit.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Simon Roberts

Simon Roberts is a British visual artist based in Brighton, UK,
recognised for his large-format tableaux photographs exploring identity,
belonging, place, history and the socio-political fabric of Britain. His
work has been exhibited widely and is held in major public and private
collections, including the George Eastman House, Deutsche Borse Art
Collection and the Victoria and Albert Museum. In 2010, he was appointed
the official British Election Artist by the House of Commons Works of Art
Committee.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Marcus Bleasdale

Marcus Bleasdale CMG is an award-winning documentary photographer,
filmmaker and human rights advocate. Over the past two decades, his work
has focused on conflict, human rights abuses and the financial networks
that sustain violence, with long-term reporting across Africa and other
conflict-affected regions. A contributing photographer for National
Geographic and long-standing collaborator with Human Rights Watch, he was
appointed Companion of the Order of St Michael and St George in 2023 for
services to international photojournalism and human rights.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Will Pryce

Will Pryce is a London-based professional photographer working
internationally for editorial and commercial clients. He specialises in
architecture, high-end residential projects, luxury hotels and resorts.
His work has appeared in publications including Wallpaper*, The World of
Interiors, Architectural Digest, Conde Nast Traveller, House & Garden,
Domus, Dwell, Icon and Country Life. He has written and photographed
several books on architectural subjects, and has photographed further
titles for Thames & Hudson, Rizzoli, the University of Chicago Press and
Scala.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Amin Khelghat

Amin Khelghat is a fine art photographer whose black-and-white work
explores motion, rhythm, form and light. Across series including Motion
Abstraction, Herd Harmony, Echoes of Light and Urban Tales, his images
move between choreography, landscape, portraiture and the city. His
photographs often use contrast, shadow and pattern to suggest solitude,
presence and mystery within ordinary moments.
