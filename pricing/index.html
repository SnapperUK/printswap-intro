Pricing

What a swap costs

You pay for one print: yours, sent to the other artist. They pay for theirs. Your total is the print price plus shipping.

Custom sizes are priced as the smallest standard size they fit inside.

Shipping depends on where you live

You pay the shipping rate for the country you live in, whoever you swap with.

Example: a UK artist swapping an 8 × 10 pays £24.50 + £15 = £39.50.

Every print comes with a certificate of authenticity. Prices are in pounds sterling; your bank converts if you pay in another currency.

When you pay

When you send a request we place a temporary hold on your card. You are only charged if the other artist accepts. If they decline, or the request is cancelled or expires, the hold is released.

If something goes wrong

Once a swap is accepted it is generally binding and goes to print. If something goes wrong, such as a duplicate payment, a technical error, a delivery failure or a print quality issue, email hello@print-swap.com. Print Swap may investigate and, where appropriate, coordinate a reprint, credit or refund, in accordance with our Terms.

Customs

Prints sent across borders may attract import duty or tax in the receiving country, payable by the person receiving them.
