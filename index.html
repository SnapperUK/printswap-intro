Most of your work will never leave a screen.

You make an image, edit it, post it, and people see it four centimetres wide on a phone before they swipe past. A few deserve better: to be printed, framed and lived with.

The same is true of the photographers you admire. Their best images are out there, and almost none of them hang on your wall.

Print Swap is for those images. Yours, and the ones you want.

How a swap works

  1. Upload an image. Pick one you’d be glad to see on someone else’s wall, then set the size and paper.
  2. Browse. Look through work from photographers and visual artists until you find a print you’d want to live with.
  3. Propose a swap. They see your image and decide in their own time. Nothing moves unless you both say yes.
  4. Both prints are made. No money passes between you: each of you pays only for your own print to be produced and posted. A professional lab prints both images as editions on archival paper and sends them out, each with a certificate of authenticity.

Why I started Print Swap

Last year, staring at an empty space on my wall, it struck me that I should have swapped more prints with photographers over the years. Like most photographers, I want to be surrounded by work that inspires and moves me, and that’s very seldom my own work.

Photographs were once objects: prints you made, kept in boxes, put on walls. Print Swap makes that exchange simple again, because good images deserve more than a few seconds in a feed.

Sam Faulkner, founder

Some images deserve a wall.
