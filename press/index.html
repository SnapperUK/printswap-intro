No buying.

Just the trade.

A non-commercial platform where photographers and visual artists swap gallery-quality physical prints. Live on iOS.

"I was standing in front of a blank wall wishing I had a photograph for it, and it occurred to me that if I'd spent the last twenty years swapping prints with photographers I know, I'd have fifty pictures by now. Print Swap is the simple infrastructure for that."

Sam Faulkner — photographer and founder

How it works

Upload

Add prints from your portfolio. See what other artists are offering.

The exchange

Offer a print. If they say yes, both go into production.

Direct trade

No sales. No commissions. No popularity contests.

Fulfilment

Every print is made to gallery standard, then sent straight to the artist.

Notes for editors

Print Swap soft launched in May 2026 and is now open worldwide. Nearly 300 photographers and visual artists have joined so far. Prints are produced and shipped from three hubs, in London, Hamburg and New York.

Print Swap was founded by photographer Sam Faulkner.
