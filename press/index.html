Key facts

  • Live on iOS — available on the App Store.
  • Nearly 300 photographers and visual artists have joined.
  • Soft launched May 2026; now open worldwide.
  • Prints are produced and shipped from three hubs: London, Hamburg, and New York.
  • Founded by photographer Sam Faulkner.

Founder

Sam Faulkner — photographer and founder

"I was standing in front of a blank wall wishing I had a photograph for it, and it occurred to me that if I'd spent the last twenty years swapping prints with photographers I know, I'd have fifty pictures by now. Print Swap is the simple infrastructure for that."

What Print Swap is not

  • Not a marketplace. Photographers and visual artists are not selling work.
  • Not a social platform. No follower counts, engagement metrics, or algorithmic feeds.
  • Not a print-on-demand service. The exchange is the point, not the transaction.
  • Not a startup story. A photographer built a tool for photographers and visual artists.
